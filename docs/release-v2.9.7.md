## What's new in 2.9.7: player 2's own controls

Fixed:

- **Player 2 couldn't change their button mapping (#33).** Players 2 and 3 used player 1's controller buttons, and the Controls page had nothing for them. It now has a tab per player (Left/Right, LB/RB or a click switches them). Each player has their own controller buttons and triggers, and keyboard keys of their own, none until you pick some, so two can share a keyboard. If you never changed player 2's buttons, they stay as they were.

Not fixed yet:

- **Sprites flickering in 7A's robot factory when it's crowded (#21).**
- **A light black flicker with high frame rate (Streets of Rage 2),** **round 6's screen unlock glitch (#20, the other parts)** and **the background jitter at the start of a level (#17).**

Everything from 2.9.6 is in this release. If you have 2.9 or later, the collection offers this update when it opens. On Linux with the native build, run the new source's `tools/install.sh` again to update.

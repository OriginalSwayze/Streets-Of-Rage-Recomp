## What's new in 2.2: Bare Knuckle III

**Bare Knuckle III**, the Japanese original of Streets of Rage 3, now plays as an edition of Streets of Rage 3, with its story, its own difficulty and the fighters' original colours. Every enhancement works in it: widescreen, high frame rate, the HD effects, cheats, combat info and the fighters' colours.

| Edition | Cartridge |
|---|---|
| Streets of Rage 3 | Streets of Rage 3 (USA) |
| Bare Knuckle III | Bare Knuckle III (Japan) |
| Bare Knuckle III (English) | Bare Knuckle III (Japan) with Twilight Translations' English patch 1.00 |

- **Setup takes Bare Knuckle III too:** on the Streets of Rage 3 row, Browse again and pick your Japanese cartridge. For it in English, also pick Twilight Translations' patch (`bk3_100.zip` or `bk3_100.ips`): Setup applies it to your ROM itself. Any one of the three is enough to install Streets of Rage 3.
- **Edition setting**, in the launcher's **Game** tab and the pause menu's **More** tab. In the pause menu it restarts in that edition from power on (unsaved progress is lost).
- **Each edition keeps its own saves,** save states and launcher art; the launcher's cover shows Bare Knuckle III's own title screen.

Also in this release, from your reports:

- **Sound at the console's own balance.** The music played about three times too loud: loud passages clipped, or were squeezed by the limiter, and the bass, the loudest part, drowned out the melody. It is now mixed as the console mixes it, with no clipping. A new **Bass** setting (launcher **Music** tab, pause menu **More** tab) makes the low end lighter or heavier, from -12 to +12 dB.
- **High frame rate: no more jitter while walking.** Every few steps a walking fighter stood still for a frame and then jumped 1-2 pixels (most visible in Streets of Rage 2). It now glides through every frame of its walk.
- **Health bars only over ordinary enemies.** Bosses get no bar of ours: the game's own HUD shows their health, and their sprites are too big for a bar over them.
- **Widescreen intros in 4:3.** Streets of Rage 2's and 3's intro and story scenes have black sides, as they were drawn for: no more Mr. X's hands waiting beside the picture. In play the screen is as wide as before.
- **Closing a game's window closes the collection too.** Before, the collection came back each time, and it looked like the launcher would not close. The launcher's **Back** and the pause menu's **Quit** still go back to the collection.
- **The launcher names the right cartridge.** In 2.1 a ROM that was not the game's showed "Not Streets of Rage (World)" in every game's launcher.
- **Streets of Rage 3's AI partner** now goes for the enemies (it looked for them by a byte that only the first game uses).
- **Streets of Rage 2, widescreen:** at the end of an area (round 2's bridge, the truck's container, the fight beside the truck) the part of the screen past the end of the map is black, instead of showing leftovers such as a piece of the truck, and the players stop at the map's end there.

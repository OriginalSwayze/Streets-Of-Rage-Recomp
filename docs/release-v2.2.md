## What's new in 2.2: Bare Knuckle III

**Bare Knuckle III**, the Japanese original of Streets of Rage 3, now plays as an edition of Streets of Rage 3, with its story, its own difficulty and the fighters' original colours. Every enhancement works in it: widescreen, high frame rate, the HD effects, cheats, combat info and the fighters' colours.

| Edition | Cartridge |
|---|---|
| Streets of Rage 3 | Streets of Rage 3 (USA) |
| Bare Knuckle III | Bare Knuckle III (Japan) |
| Bare Knuckle III (English) | Bare Knuckle III (Japan) with Twilight Translations' English patch 1.00 |

- **Setup takes Bare Knuckle III too:** on the Streets of Rage 3 row, Browse again and pick your Japanese cartridge. For it in English, also pick Twilight Translations' patch (`bk3_100.zip` or `bk3_100.ips`): Setup applies it to your ROM itself. Any one of the three is enough to install Streets of Rage 3.
- **Edition setting**, in the launcher's **Game** tab and the pause menu's **More** tab. In the pause menu it restarts in that edition from power on (unsaved progress is lost).
- **Each edition keeps its own saves,** save states and launcher art; the launcher's cover shows Bare Knuckle III's own title screen.

Also in this release:

- **Streets of Rage 2, widescreen:** at the end of an area (round 2's bridge, the truck's container, the fight beside the truck) the part of the screen past the end of the map is black, instead of showing leftovers such as a piece of the truck, and the players stop at the map's end there.

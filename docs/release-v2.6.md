## What's new in 2.6: a third player in Streets of Rage 2 (experimental)

**Three people can fight on the same street in Streets of Rage 2.** Turn on **Third player** (launcher **Cheats** tab, or the pause menu's), plug in three controllers, and start a round: Start on the third controller brings in a third fighter, one neither player is using, with that fighter's own moves and colours. Enemies go for the third fighter and can hit them, and they have lives like the others; out of lives, Start brings them back in. It is off by default.

This is a first version, tried with scripted input rather than three real controllers, so expect rough edges:

- The third fighter has no health bar or score on screen yet.
- With it on, one fewer enemy can be on screen at a time (five, not six).
- Only Streets of Rage 2 has it so far.
- Save states and rewind have not been tried with it, nor the last stages, the final boss or the ending. After loading a state, its picture may be wrong until its next move.
- Fighters' attacks can hit it, as they hit each other in a two-player game, on the levels where the game allows that.

Everything from 2.5.2 is in this release.

## What's new in 2.3.1

- **Bare Knuckle III names Sammy.** The launcher's and pause menu's **Colours** pages call the fourth fighter Sammy, as the Japanese original does (Skate in Streets of Rage 3).
- **Widescreen, Streets of Rage 2 round 8:** the building's rooms are one screen each. Mr. X's room no longer shows the lift door beside it: its sides are blurred (or black), as the room has nothing past its walls.
- **Widescreen, Streets of Rage 3 round 3's bar:** the room ends short of the game's map blocks; the view now slides to the bar's door and wall instead of showing a blue patch past its end, and the HUD's row shows the room beside the HUD.

Checked in widescreen with every round of Streets of Rage and Streets of Rage 2, and Streets of Rage 3 to round 4, playing through with the test autopilot.

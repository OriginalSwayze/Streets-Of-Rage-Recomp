## What's new in 2.2.1: widescreen edges

**Widescreen: no more leftovers past the ends of a map.** Where an area's map starts or ends, the widescreen columns past it showed whatever had been drawn there before: the backdrop colour and pieces of other scenery, as in a report from Streets of Rage 2. Those columns are now black until the camera moves on, and the players stop at the map's end.

- **Streets of Rage 2:** at the start of every area (each round's start, round 1's bar and the alley after it, and the others like them), as well as at their ends (fixed in 2.2).
- **Streets of Rage 3 and Bare Knuckle III:** at the end of every area (at the end of round 1's first area, part of the next one showed), as well as at their starts (already black before).
- **Streets of Rage:** checked at the start and end of round 1, where the game's own street fills the widescreen columns: nothing to hide there.

Everything from 2.2 is in this release too: Bare Knuckle III as an edition of Streets of Rage 3, sound at the console's own balance with a Bass setting, smooth walking at high frame rates, no health bars over bosses, 4:3 intros in widescreen, and closing the collection with a game's window.
